COGNITIVE EXPERIMENT

Memory & Encoding Study

A short study of how different encoding strategies affect free recall.

Welcome

You will complete the study in a fixed sequence of steps. Read the instructions on each screen before selecting its button; the page will tell you exactly what happens next.

  1. Study 18 words across three six-word blocks. Each block uses one encoding strategy.
  2. Count backward by threes for 30 seconds.
  3. Recall as many studied words as you can, in any order.

The three strategies used during the study are:

  • Maintenance rehearsal
  • Self-reference
  • Mental imagery

The order of strategies and the assignment of word lists are randomized for each session. Each word appears individually for a few seconds. After the study blocks, you will complete a 30-second counting task and then recall as many words as you can.

Important: Complete the task without looking back at earlier screens or using notes. Your responses are not sent to a server; they remain in this browser session unless you download the CSV files.

What to do next

You will have a text box in which to enter as many of the 18 words from the study as you remember. You may enter them in any order. Do not use notes or return to previous screens.

FREE RECALL

Recall the studied words

Your task: Type as many of the 18 words from the study as you can remember. You may recall them in any order.

Separate each word with a comma, a space, or a new line. Enter each word only once. Do not guess, use notes, or go back to earlier screens.

When you have entered all the words you can remember, select Submit recall. You will then see your results.

Spelling variations are accepted when the intended word is unambiguous. Each studied word can receive credit only once.
